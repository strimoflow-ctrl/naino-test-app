🔬 1. Alpha-Particle Scattering Experiment

Rutherford's gold foil experiment showed that most of the space inside the atom is empty and the entire positive charge and most of the mass is concentrated in a tiny central core called the Nucleus.

  • Distance of closest approach (r0): The minimum distance from the nucleus up to which an α-particle can approach. At this distance, its entire Kinetic Energy converts to Electrostatic Potential Energy. r0 ∝ 1/K.
  • Impact Parameter (b): The perpendicular distance of the initial velocity vector of the α-particle from the central line of the nucleus. Smaller the 'b', larger the scattering angle. If b=0, scattering angle is 180° (head-on collision).

⚛️ 2. Bohr's Model of Hydrogen Atom

Bohr modified Rutherford's model using Planck's quantum theory.

Bohr's Postulates:

  1. Electrons revolve in certain stable, non-radiating orbits (Stationary Orbits).
  2. Quantization of Angular Momentum: An electron can only revolve in those orbits for which its orbital angular momentum is an integral multiple of h/2π.
    mvr = nh / 2π
  3. Energy is radiated or absorbed only when an electron jumps from one orbit to another.
    hν = Ef - Ei
💡 Important Bohr Formulas (for H-like atoms)

Radius: rn = 0.529 (n2 / Z) Å
Velocity: vn = 2.18 × 106 (Z / n) m/s
Energy: En = -13.6 (Z2 / n2) eV

⚡ 3. Energy Levels of Hydrogen

The energy of an electron in the nth orbit of hydrogen (Z=1) is En = -13.6 / n2 eV.

  • n=1 (Ground state): E1 = -13.6 eV
  • n=2 (First excited state): E2 = -3.4 eV
  • n=3 (Second excited state): E3 = -1.51 eV
  • n=4: E4 = -0.85 eV
🚨 Exam Facts: Energy Relations

Total Energy (TE) = - Kinetic Energy (KE)
Potential Energy (PE) = 2 × Total Energy (TE)

If TE = -13.6 eV, then KE = +13.6 eV and PE = -27.2 eV.

🌈 4. Hydrogen Emission Spectrum

When hydrogen gas is excited, electrons jump to higher levels. When they fall back, they emit photons of specific wavelengths, forming a line spectrum.

Rydberg Formula: 1/λ = R Z2 [1/n12 - 1/n22] (where R ≈ 1.097 × 107 m-1)

Fig: Energy Level Diagram and Spectral Series of Hydrogen

Spectral Series:

  • Lyman: Jumps to n=1. Region: Ultraviolet (UV).
  • Balmer: Jumps to n=2. Region: Visible.
  • Paschen: Jumps to n=3. Region: Infrared (IR).
  • Brackett (n=4) & Pfund (n=5): Region: Far Infrared.
