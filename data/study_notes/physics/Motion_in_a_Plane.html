📐 1. Vectors

Physical quantities are classified into scalars and vectors.

  • Scalars: Only have magnitude (e.g., mass, time, speed).
  • Vectors: Have both magnitude and direction, and obey triangle law of addition (e.g., displacement, velocity, force).
  • Unit Vector (a): A vector of unit magnitude pointing in the direction of a given vector. a = A / |A|. (e.g., i, j, k).

Vector Addition & Resolution

Parallelogram Law: If two vectors A and B are represented by two adjacent sides of a parallelogram, their resultant is given by the diagonal passing through their intersection.

R = √(A2 + B2 + 2AB cosθ)

Resolution: A vector A making an angle θ with x-axis can be resolved into components: Ax = A cosθ and Ay = A sinθ.

💡 Key Formula: Dot and Cross Product

Dot Product (Scalar): A • B = AB cosθ
Cross Product (Vector): |A × B| = AB sinθ (Direction given by Right Hand Rule).

🚀 2. Projectile Motion

An object that is in flight after being thrown or projected is called a projectile. In the absence of air resistance, the projectile moves with a constant horizontal velocity and a constant vertical downward acceleration (g).

Suppose a particle is projected with initial velocity u at an angle θ with the horizontal:

  • Horizontal component: ux = u cosθ (remains constant)
  • Vertical component: uy = u sinθ (changes due to gravity)
Fig: Path of a Projectile

🧮 3. Formulas for Projectile

💡 Important Equations
  • Equation of Trajectory: y = x tanθ - (gx2) / (2u2cos2θ)
  • Time of Flight (T): T = (2u sinθ) / g
  • Maximum Height (H): H = (u2sin2θ) / 2g
  • Horizontal Range (R): R = (u2sin2θ) / g
🚨 Exam Facts: Maximum Range

The horizontal range is maximum when the angle of projection is 45°.
Rmax = u2 / g.
Also, the range is the same for two complementary angles of projection: θ and (90° - θ).

🔄 4. Uniform Circular Motion

When an object follows a circular path at a constant speed, the motion is called uniform circular motion.

  • Angular Velocity (ω): Rate of change of angular displacement. ω = Δθ / Δt. Unit: rad/s.
  • Relation between v and ω: v = rω (where v is linear speed and r is radius).
  • Centripetal Acceleration (ac): Even though speed is constant, velocity is changing due to continuous change in direction. The acceleration is directed towards the centre.
💡 Centripetal Acceleration

ac = v2 / r = ω2r

🚣 5. Relative Velocity in 2D

The velocity of object A relative to object B is: vAB = vA - vB.

River-Boat Problem

If a boat moves with velocity vbr (velocity of boat relative to river) and river flows with velocity vr, absolute velocity of boat is vb = vbr + vr.

  • To cross in shortest time: Head perpendicular to river flow. Time = d / vbr.
  • To cross along shortest path: Head at an angle upstream such that horizontal component of vbr cancels vr. Velocity = √(vbr2 - vr2).

Rain-Man Problem

If rain falls vertically with velocity vr and man walks horizontally with velocity vm, velocity of rain relative to man is vrm = vr - vm. The umbrella should be held in the direction of -vrm to avoid getting wet.
