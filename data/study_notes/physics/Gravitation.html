🌍 1. Universal Law of Gravitation

Every particle in the universe attracts every other particle with a force that is directly proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between their centres.

💡 Gravitational Force

F = G (m1m2) / r2

Where G is the Universal Gravitational Constant: G = 6.67 × 10-11 N m2 / kg2.

Vector Form: F12 = - [G(m1m2)/r2] r12

  • Gravitational force is always attractive.
  • It is a central force (acts along the line joining the centres).
  • It obeys Newton's Third Law (F12 = -F21).
  • It is independent of the intervening medium.

⏱️ 2. Kepler's Laws of Planetary Motion

Johannes Kepler formulated three laws describing the motion of planets around the Sun:

  1. Law of Orbits: Every planet revolves around the sun in an elliptical orbit with the sun situated at one of the foci.
  2. Law of Areas: The line joining the planet to the sun sweeps out equal areas in equal intervals of time. (Areal velocity is constant). This law is a consequence of the Conservation of Angular Momentum.
  3. Law of Periods: The square of the time period of revolution of a planet is proportional to the cube of the semi-major axis of the elliptical orbit.

⬇️ 3. Acceleration Due to Gravity (g)

The acceleration produced in a freely falling body under the gravitational pull of the earth.

g = GMe / Re2 ≈ 9.8 m/s2 on Earth's surface.

Variation of g:

  • With Height (h): gh = g (1 - 2h/Re) for h << Re. Decreases with height.
  • With Depth (d): gd = g (1 - d/Re). Decreases linearly with depth. At the centre of earth, g = 0.
  • With Shape of Earth: Earth is an oblate spheroid (equatorial radius > polar radius). Hence, g is maximum at poles and minimum at equator.
  • With Rotation: g′ = g - Reω2cos2λ (where λ is latitude). Decreases due to rotation.

🔋 4. Gravitational Potential Energy & Potential

Gravitational Potential Energy (U)

The work done in bringing a mass 'm' from infinity to a point in the gravitational field of mass 'M'.

💡 Formula

U = - GMm / r

Note: It is always negative and becomes zero at infinity.

Gravitational Potential (V)

Amount of work done in bringing a unit mass from infinity to that point. V = - GM / r.

🚀 5. Escape Velocity & Satellites

Escape Velocity (ve)

The minimum velocity with which a body should be projected from the surface of the earth so that it escapes from the earth's gravitational field.

ve = √(2GM/R) = √(2gR)

For Earth, ve ≈ 11.2 km/s. It is independent of the mass of the projected body and the angle of projection.

Orbital Velocity (vo)

The velocity required to put a satellite into its orbit around the earth.

vo = √(GM/r)

For a satellite orbiting very close to earth (r ≈ R): vo = √(gR) ≈ 7.92 km/s.

🚨 Exam Facts: Energy of a Satellite

For a satellite in circular orbit:
Kinetic Energy: K = GMm / 2r
Potential Energy: U = - GMm / r
Total Energy: E = - GMm / 2r = - K = U/2
Total energy is negative, which implies a bound system.
