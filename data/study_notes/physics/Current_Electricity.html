🔌 1. Electric Current and Drift Velocity

Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge. I = dq / dt.

Drift Velocity (vd)

The average velocity with which free electrons get drifted towards the positive end of the conductor under the influence of an external electric field.

vd = (eE / m) τ (where τ is relaxation time)

💡 Relation between I and vd

I = n e A vd

Where n = number density of free electrons, A = Area of cross section. Mobility (μ) = vd / E.

⚡ 2. Ohm's Law and Resistance

At constant temperature, the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across its ends. V ∝ I ⇒ V = IR.

Resistance (R): R = ρ L / A. (where ρ is resistivity).
Also, R = m L / (n e2 τ A), so ρ = m / (n e2 τ).

🚨 Exam Facts: Stretching a Wire

If a wire is stretched to 'n' times its original length (volume constant):
New Length L′ = nL, New Area A′ = A/n.
New Resistance R′ = n2 R.

Temperature Dependence of Resistance

RT = R0 [1 + α(T - T0)], where α is the temperature coefficient of resistance. For metals, α is positive. For semiconductors, α is negative.

🔋 3. Cells, EMF, and Internal Resistance

Electromotive Force (EMF, E): The potential difference across the terminals of a cell when no current is drawn from it.

Internal Resistance (r): Resistance offered by the electrolyte of the cell.

Terminal Voltage (V): V = E - Ir (during discharging).
During charging: V = E + Ir.

Grouping of Cells

  • Series: Eeq = E1 + E2, req = r1 + r2. Max current when R >> req.
  • Parallel: Eeq = (E1/r1 + E2/r2) / (1/r1 + 1/r2). Max current when req >> R.

⚖️ 4. Kirchhoff's Laws

Fig: Kirchhoff's Current Law (Junction Rule)
  • Junction Rule (KCL): The algebraic sum of currents meeting at a junction is zero (ΣI = 0). Based on Conservation of Charge.
  • Loop Rule (KVL): The algebraic sum of changes in potential around any closed loop is zero (ΣΔV = 0). Based on Conservation of Energy.

🌉 5. Wheatstone Bridge and Instruments

An arrangement of four resistances used to measure an unknown resistance.

Balanced condition: No current flows through the galvanometer. P / Q = R / S.

Meter Bridge

Practical application of Wheatstone bridge. Works on the principle of balanced Wheatstone bridge.
Unknown resistance S = R [ (100 - l) / l ].

📘 Potentiometer (Often skipped but useful concept)

Measures EMF accurately because it draws no current from the cell at the null point.
Comparison of EMFs: E1 / E2 = l1 / l2.
Internal Resistance: r = R [ (l1 - l2) / l2 ].
